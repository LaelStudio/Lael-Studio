08 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Why is the domain included? +

Your first year domain is included in every package, so your new site has a professional home from day one.

How and when do I renew my domain? +

We'll send you a simple renewal guide. Domains renew annually at the registrar's current rate, usually around $15–$30 anually.

What's SEO, and why is it free? +

SEO helps search engines understand your website. We include the essential setup in every build because it should be standard, not a luxury.

Will you update my website after final
payment?+

Our packages include the agreed revisions before launch. Ongoing updates can be seperately requested at an additional fee by emailing us.

Why is there a 10% deposit? +

It secures your request and covers the initial domain cost and project setup work.

Can I use the website preview? +

No. Previews are watermarked and private while your project is in progress. Your live site is delivered after final payment.

What's your refund policy? +

Strictly no refunds. When the client settles their outstanding amount, it is an agreement that they are happy with the final result. Once the final website has been accepted, we can't refund the working hours already invested nor the costs to build it.
