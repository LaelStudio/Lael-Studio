08 / FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Why is the domain included? +

Your first year domain is included in every package, so your new site has a professional home from day one.

How and when do I renew my domain? +

We'll send you a simple renewal guide. Domains renew annually at the registrar's current rate, usually around $15–$30.

What's SEO, and why is it free? +

SEO helps search engines understand your website. We include the essential setup in every build because it should be standard, not a luxury.

Will you update my website after final payment? +

Our packages include the agreed revisions before launch. Ongoing updates can be requested separately by emailing us.

Why is there a $15 deposit? +

It secures your request and covers the initial domain and project setup work.

Can I use the website preview? +

No. Previews are watermarked and private while your project is in progress. Your live site is delivered after final payment.

What's your refund policy? +

Once the final website has been accepted, we can't refund the working hours already invested.
